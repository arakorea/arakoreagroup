OFFICIAL ECOSYSTEM OVERVIEW

ARA KOREA GROUP ECOSYSTEM

ARA KOREA GROUP is a connected creative business network spanning culture, arts, intellectual property, music, records, publishing, production, performance, artists, media, jewelry, creator platforms, live experiences, community and commerce. The group is organized around four principal worlds: ARA KOREA, AK ENM, TIKOONZ and OURBANG.

Culture & ArtsARA KOREA develops cultural and artistic projects and connects them with commerce and film-festival activity.
IP & EntertainmentAK ENM develops intellectual property and operates across music, records, publishing, production and performance.
Artists, Media & CommerceTIKOONZ connects artists, music, media, jewelry and commerce across domestic and global channels.
Creators & PlatformsOURBANG is designed as an expandable creator-centered environment for discovery, live activity, content, community and commerce.
